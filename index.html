Cloud AI Platform

Intelligent Cloud Management and Provisioning Across AWS, Azure, GCP and Oracle cloud

1. Executive Summary

Cloud AI is a proposed intelligent cloud management and provisioning platform designed to simplify operations, optimize costs, and empower users across multiple Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) like AWS, Azure, and GCP.

Its core innovation lies in an AI agent that generates precise, editable, and cost-effective infrastructure deployment plans based on user requirements. The platform offers both AI-assisted and simplified provisioning methods, centralized multi-cloud billing analysis, and robust user management.

2. Core Problems Cloud AI Solves

Multi-Cloud Complexity

Manually managing resources and deployments across different CSPs requires specialized knowledge for each platform.

Cost Optimization

Unexpected cloud bills and inefficient resource allocation are common, and implementing cost-saving measures across multiple clouds is difficult.

Skill Gap & Time

Not all teams have deep expertise in cloud architecture or IaC. Manual provisioning is often slow and error-prone.

3. Proposed Solution & Key Features

AI-Powered Provisioning

Users describe needs in natural language, and the AI generates a complete list of required resources, a visual workflow, and cost estimations.

Unified Dashboard

A single pane of glass for multi-cloud billing analysis, project management, and resource overview.

Billing Aggregation & Analysis

Connects and analyzes billing data from AWS, Azure, and GCP, visualizing spending trends per CSP and service.
